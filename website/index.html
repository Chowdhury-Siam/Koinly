THE DETAILS

Good questions, clear answers.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Choose Use offline and start with local data. If you later decide to sync devices, you can connect your own self-hosted Worker.

Is the live demo connected to the real app?

No. The demo is a self-contained browser simulation with sample transactions and accounts. You can explore its main workflows without using your own finances. Reset it any time.

Can I use Koinly on more than one device?

Yes, with optional self-hosted sync using your own Cloudflare Worker and Turso database. Offline-only installations keep their data on their respective devices.

Can I back up and export my data?

Koinly supports encrypted local backup files and analytics exports in PDF, XLSX and TXT. The app also supports optional configured Telegram and Google Drive delivery via your own Worker.

Where can I download Koinly?

Android and Windows store listings are planned. Their links will appear here when published. The source project also documents Linux and macOS builds.
